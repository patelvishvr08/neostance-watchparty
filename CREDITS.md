## Quiet Orbit and Amber Tides

Original procedural motion studies included with Afterhours. No external images, footage or music. The geometry and color fields are generated by `scripts/generate-ambient.py`. Released under CC0; you may modify and reuse them. They are intentionally silent and labelled as motion studies.

## Fonts

DM Sans and Instrument Serif from Google Fonts. Their original SIL Open Font License notices are included in `public/fonts/`. Font files are bundled for offline use. Font sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif.

## Dependencies

React/React DOM, Lucide icons, Vite, and the maintained hosting starter retain their package licenses. Lucide is used for interface icons. No external paid API or movie recommendation service is required. Recommendations in this project are a small tagged catalogue with filters and fuzzy search; they are not AI-generated or based on personal viewing history.

## Manrope

Manrope is used for the updated interface. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope . The SIL Open Font License is included in `public/fonts/Manrope-OFL.txt`.

## Under the Floodlights

Original procedural stadium animation created for Afterhours using `scripts/generate-stadium.py`. It depicts an illustrated football scene, not footage of an actual tournament. The supplied animation is released under CC0. No Pexels footage is bundled.

## Neostance display typography

Montserrat is used for the Neostance wordmark and display hierarchy. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/montserrat . Its SIL Open Font License is bundled in `public/fonts/Montserrat-OFL.txt`. Manrope remains the interface text face.
